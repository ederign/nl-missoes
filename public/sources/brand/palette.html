Usage rules

  • Use white or cream for the main page and large sections. Use soft green for small support panels.
  • Reserve mission green and lime for branding, illustration, large display lettering, and accents.
  • Use action green for white button labels and text links. Bright green is not the default background for white body-sized labels.
  • Use ink for body text and headings. Keep dark colors in text and icons, not large page backgrounds.
  • Soft borders are decorative; form fields need stronger outlines and a visible action-green focus indicator.
